## Reading (mechanical)
Steering ŵ to flip the linear probe's readout on ~99% of spans (both directions) moves behavior by **~1.3% of the role effect** (0.0506 / 7.83), and **a random variance-matched direction moves it the same or more.** M ≈ 0 with a CI tight around zero. ⇒ **The provenance direction the model REPRESENTS at 95.5% (PRV-01c, TV≥0.91) is NOT on the causal path to its behavioral role-arbitration.** Pre-committed demotion (§0.4): "the model can tell" → "provenance is *present in the residual stream*," not "*available to its behavior* on this axis."

The strong behavioral role-effect B = −3.915 (user-obeying) is real but runs through **something other than ŵ** — consistent with the Gate-0 flags (baseline compliance asymmetry −2.83; role/recency confound), i.e. B may be baseline-compliance + recency rather than provenance-driven arbitration.

## SCOPE — pre-registered (§0.9, §7), load-bearing, NOT discovered post-hoc
